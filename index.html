Our Standard

What every clean includes

Each standard visit follows the same room by room checklist. Deep cleans go further with baseboards, fixtures, and built up grime.

Kitchen

  • Counters, backsplash, and sink
  • Stovetop and appliance exteriors
  • Microwave inside and out
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped

Bathrooms

  • Toilets cleaned and sanitized
  • Showers, tubs, and sinks scrubbed
  • Mirrors and fixtures polished
  • Floors washed

Living Areas

  • Surfaces and decor dusted
  • Carpets and rugs vacuumed
  • Hard floors mopped
  • Trash emptied, cushions tidied

Bedrooms

  • Beds made, linens changed on request
  • Furniture and shelves dusted
  • Mirrors and glass cleaned
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before your first clean. Still have a question? We are happy to help.

Contact Us
What areas do you serve?

We serve North Orange County and surrounding areas, especially Brea, Yorba Linda, Diamond Bar, and Walnut, along with Fullerton, Placentia, La Habra, Anaheim Hills, Chino Hills, and Rowland Heights. If you are just outside these areas, contact us and we will do our best to help.

Do you bring your own cleaning supplies?

Yes. We arrive with professional supplies and equipment. If you would like us to use specific products in your home, just let us know.

How is pricing determined?

Every space is different, so we quote based on the size of your home or business, its condition, and the service you choose. You will always receive a clear price before any work begins.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

No. Many clients share a door code, lockbox, or spare key. We confirm when we arrive and let you know as soon as the clean is complete.

Can I book a one-time cleaning?

Absolutely. Many clients start with a one-time standard or deep clean, and you can switch to a recurring plan at any time.

What if something was missed?

Your satisfaction matters to us. If anything is not right, contact us within 24 hours and we will come back to take care of it.
