About Us

A North Orange County cleaning company built on trust

Prime Pure Cleaning is a locally owned business serving homes and workplaces across North Orange County and surrounding areas, led by owner Mika Pavy.

Our Story

Cleaning that gives you your time back

We started Prime Pure Cleaning because we believe everyone deserves to come home to a space that feels calm, fresh, and cared for.

Life in North Orange County is busy. Between work, family, and everything in between, cleaning is often the first thing to slip. Our job is to take it off your plate completely, with a level of detail and consistency you can count on.

As a locally owned company, we care about our reputation in the community we live in. That means showing up when we say we will, communicating clearly, and treating every home and business as if it were our own.

Our Approach

A consistent process, every single visit

Great cleaning is not about rushing through a list. It is about knowing what matters in each room, using the right products for each surface, and checking the work before we leave.

  • Room by room checklists tailored to your space
  • Professional grade supplies and equipment
  • Your preferences noted and followed every time
  • A final walkthrough before we leave
