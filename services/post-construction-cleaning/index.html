Post-Construction Cleaning

Renovations and new builds leave behind fine dust and residue that ordinary cleaning cannot handle. We turn a finished job site into a space that is truly ready to move into.

Book Now (714) 706-1203
Overview

From job site to move-in ready

Construction dust settles everywhere: inside cabinets, on top of door frames, in light fixtures, and deep in window tracks. We work from top to bottom with the right equipment and microfiber, removing dust in stages so it does not simply resettle on clean surfaces.

We also take care of the finishing details: removing stickers and labels, wiping away paint specks and adhesive residue, and polishing new fixtures and glass. It is the final step that lets your new space look the way it was designed to.

What is included

  • Fine construction dust removed from all surfaces
  • Vents, fans, and light fixtures cleaned
  • Inside cabinets, drawers, and shelving
  • Stickers, labels, and adhesive residue removed
  • Paint specks and overspray cleaned from fixtures
  • Interior windows, sills, and tracks
  • New appliances and fixtures polished
  • Floors vacuumed, mopped, and detailed
  • Final detail wipe before handover

Questions

Post-Construction Cleaning FAQs

Have a question we did not cover? Call, text, or send us a message and we will be glad to help.

When should I schedule a post-construction clean?

Once the main work is finished and the trades are out of the space. If touch-ups are still happening, we can schedule a rough clean now and a final detail clean before handover.

Do you work directly with contractors?

Yes. We are happy to coordinate with your contractor or project manager on timing, access, and scope.

Do you haul away construction debris?

Our service focuses on cleaning: dust, residue, and small debris. Large debris, leftover materials, and dumpster loads should be removed by your contractor before we begin.
