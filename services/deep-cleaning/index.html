Overview

When your home needs more than a tidy

Over time, dust, grease, and soap scum build up in places a quick clean never reaches. Our deep cleaning service targets those areas directly: baseboards, door frames, light fixtures, vents, grout lines, and the grime that collects around appliances and fixtures.

We work through every room methodically, using the right tools and products for each surface. The result is a home that looks brighter, smells fresher, and is far easier to keep clean afterward.

What is included

  • Everything in our standard clean
  • Baseboards, doors, and door frames hand wiped
  • Light switches, outlets, and handles sanitized
  • Ceiling fans, vents, and light fixtures dusted
  • Stovetop, range hood, and backsplash degreased
  • Shower glass, tile, and grout scrubbed
  • Faucets and fixtures descaled and polished
  • Cabinet fronts and handles wiped
  • Behind and under movable furniture cleaned

Questions

Deep Cleaning FAQs

Have a question we did not cover? Call, text, or send us a message and we will be glad to help.

How often should I schedule a deep clean?

Most homes benefit from a deep clean every three to six months, with standard cleans in between. It is also the best starting point before a recurring plan.

How long does a deep clean take?

Usually longer than a standard visit, often four to seven hours depending on size and condition. Larger homes may have a team of two or more.

Does a deep clean include the inside of the oven and fridge?

Inside the oven and refrigerator are available as add-ons with any deep clean. Just mention them when you request your quote.
