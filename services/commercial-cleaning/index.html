Commercial Cleaning

A clean workplace makes a strong first impression and a healthier environment for your team. We provide dependable cleaning for offices, retail spaces, and small businesses, on a schedule that fits your hours.

Get a Free Quote (626) 555-0199
Overview

A workplace that reflects your standards

Your clients notice the details: the lobby glass, the restrooms, the break room, and the floors. We build a cleaning plan around the way your space is used, focusing on high traffic areas and the touchpoints people use every day.

We can clean before opening, after closing, or on weekends so your team is never interrupted. You will have a clear scope of work, consistent results, and a single point of contact for anything you need.

What is included

  • Desks, workstations, and shared surfaces
  • Restrooms cleaned, sanitized, and restocked
  • Break rooms and kitchenettes
  • Lobby, reception, and entry glass
  • High-touch points disinfected
  • Trash and recycling removed
  • Carpets vacuumed and hard floors mopped
  • Conference rooms reset
  • Daily, weekly, or custom schedules

Questions

Commercial Cleaning FAQs

Have a question we did not cover? Call, text, or send us a message and we will be glad to help.

Can you clean outside of business hours?

Yes. We regularly clean early mornings, evenings, and weekends so your team and customers are never disrupted.

Do you offer a walkthrough before quoting?

For most commercial spaces we recommend a quick walkthrough. It helps us understand your layout and priorities so the quote and schedule are accurate.

Can the cleaning plan change over time?

Of course. As your business grows or your needs change, we adjust the scope and frequency to match.
