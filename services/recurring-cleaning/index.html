Overview

A clean home that stays that way

Recurring cleaning is the easiest way to keep your home looking its best. Instead of booking each visit, you choose a schedule that suits your household and we arrive on the same day each cycle, ready to work through your checklist.

Because we get to know your home over time, each visit becomes more efficient and more personal. We remember your preferences, focus on the areas that matter most to you, and rotate detail tasks so your whole home stays fresh.

What is included

  • Weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly scheduling
  • A consistent checklist tailored to your home
  • Your preferences and priorities kept on file
  • Rotating detail tasks each visit
  • Easy rescheduling with advance notice
  • Add-ons available on any visit
  • Priority booking for extra or holiday cleans

Questions

Recurring Cleaning Plans FAQs

Have a question we did not cover? Call, text, or send us a message and we will be glad to help.

Which schedule is right for me?

Weekly suits busy families and homes with pets. Bi-weekly is the most popular choice for most households. Monthly works well for smaller homes or anyone who keeps up with light cleaning in between.

Can I skip or reschedule a visit?

Yes. Just let us know with reasonable notice and we will move your visit to a time that works for you.

Do I need a deep clean first?

If your home has not been professionally cleaned recently, we usually recommend an initial deep clean. It sets a clean baseline so each recurring visit can focus on upkeep.
