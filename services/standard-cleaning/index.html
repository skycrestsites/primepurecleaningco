Standard House Cleaning

Dependable, detail focused cleaning for the rooms you live in every day. We take care of the dusting, floors, kitchen, and bathrooms so you can enjoy a fresh home without giving up your weekend.

Overview

A fresh, comfortable home, without the chores

Our standard clean is designed for homes that need regular attention rather than a full reset. Every visit follows the same room by room checklist, so the results are consistent and nothing important gets skipped.

We bring our own professional supplies and equipment, work efficiently around your schedule, and finish with a final walkthrough to make sure every room looks right. It is the ideal choice on its own or as part of a recurring plan.

What is included

  • Dusting of reachable surfaces, furniture, and decor
  • Vacuuming of carpets and rugs
  • Mopping of hard floors throughout
  • Kitchen counters, backsplash, and sink cleaned
  • Appliance exteriors and microwave wiped
  • Toilets, tubs, showers, and sinks sanitized
  • Mirrors and glass fixtures polished
  • Trash emptied and liners replaced
  • Beds made and common areas tidied

Questions

Standard House Cleaning FAQs

Have a question we did not cover? Call, text, or send us a message and we will be glad to help.

How long does a standard clean take?

Most homes take between two and four hours, depending on size, layout, and condition. We will give you a time estimate along with your quote.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

Not at all. Many clients share a door code, lockbox, or spare key. We will confirm when we arrive and let you know when the clean is complete.

What is the difference between standard and deep cleaning?

A standard clean maintains a home that is regularly cared for. A deep clean goes further with baseboards, door frames, fixtures, appliance buildup, and other detail work. If your home has not been professionally cleaned in a while, we usually recommend starting with a deep clean.
