Move-In and Move-Out Cleaning

Moving is stressful enough. We give empty homes a thorough, inspection ready clean so you can return the keys with confidence or start fresh in your new place.

Overview

Ready for the next chapter

An empty home is the best opportunity to clean everything properly. We work inside cabinets, drawers, closets, and appliances, and give extra attention to the areas landlords and property managers check most closely.

Whether you are moving out and want to leave the home in excellent condition, preparing a rental for new tenants, or getting your new home ready before the boxes arrive, we make sure the space is clean, fresh, and ready to live in.

What is included

  • Inside cabinets, drawers, and shelving
  • Inside oven, refrigerator, and microwave
  • Baseboards, doors, and door frames
  • Window sills and window tracks
  • Closets and interior storage spaces
  • Bathrooms fully scrubbed and sanitized
  • Light fixtures, switches, and outlets wiped
  • Floors vacuumed, mopped, and detailed
  • Garage sweep available on request

Questions

Move-In and Move-Out Cleaning FAQs

Have a question we did not cover? Call, text, or send us a message and we will be glad to help.

Does the home need to be empty?

For the best results, yes. An empty home lets us reach inside every cabinet and closet and clean floors edge to edge. If a few items will remain, just let us know in advance.

Can you clean on the same day as my move?

Often, yes, depending on availability. We recommend booking as soon as you know your moving dates so we can reserve the time.

Do you work with landlords and property managers?

Absolutely. We are happy to coordinate access, work around turnover timelines, and provide consistent results across multiple units.
